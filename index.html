Good questions

Before you go.

When can I book?

Our first cabins open in Sagana in early 2027, with Naro Moru following mid-year. Join the waitlist and you'll get first pick of dates before bookings open to everyone.

Where exactly are the cabins?

On private farms and forest land around Sagana and Naro Moru. We share the exact location and directions once you've booked, so each place stays quiet.

Is there wifi or phone signal?

There's no wifi, on purpose. Phone signal varies by site, and we'll tell you what to expect before you arrive.

What should I bring?

Food, clothes for cool evenings, and the people you'd like to switch off with. Linen, towels, firewood to start, cookware and toiletries are provided. Or add a provisions basket and bring even less.

How do I get there?

By car. Sagana is under two hours from Nairobi and Naro Moru is a comfortable half-day. Roads are good, and we'll send clear directions for the last stretch.

Is it good for the land?

Cabins sit on skids, not foundations, so nothing is permanently built. They run on solar and rainwater, with composting toilets. And every night you stay pays the family who owns the land.

Is there a minimum stay?

Two nights. It takes that long to properly slow down.
